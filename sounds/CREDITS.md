## Original libraries

- **SM Drums** by Scott McLean, Tod Stillwell and Suleiman Ali — Public Domain.
- **Big Rusty Drums**, **Frankensnare**, **Unruly Drums**, **Gogodze Phu Vol II** by Karoryfer Samples (D Smolken) — CC0 1.0.
- Vintage rhythm machine samples (rhythm box, 80s studio) from oramics/sampled, marked Public Domain.
- Breaks (`breaks/`, `breaks hifi/`) were played and recorded in PULSAR on those kits; the designed sounds were synthesized here. Both CC0.

Brand names of the original machines are not used in PULSAR.
