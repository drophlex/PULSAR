PULSAR — a drum machine for broken beats · Dark Funk Durcal design · built at Polaris Sound Lab
Free and open source (AGPL-3.0) · https://github.com/drophlex/PULSAR

INSTALL (Mac, Apple Silicon or Intel, macOS 11+)
  Easiest: open Terminal and paste
      curl -fsSL https://drophlex.github.io/PULSAR/install.sh | bash
  Or: unzip PULSAR-mac.zip, right-click "Install PULSAR.command" → Open → Open.
      If macOS refuses: System Settings → Privacy & Security → "Open Anyway".
  Then Ableton Live → Settings → Plug-ins → turn on "Use VST3 Plug-in System Folders" (or Audio Units) → Rescan.
  Browser: Plug-ins → Polaris Sound Lab → PULSAR. Drop it on a MIDI track.

IN ABLETON
  - Press play in Live: PULSAR plays with Live's tempo and lands on Live's bar (loops and jumps too).
    With Live stopped, PULSAR's own PLAY button works.
  - MIDI notes C1 (36) to D#2 (51) play the 16 pads, from a clip or a pad controller. With REC on they go into the pattern.
  - 174 automatable parameters: master, reverb, delay, the 16 perform FX as on/off switches, the pattern,
    and level, pan, tune, decay, cutoff, reso, drive and sends for each of the 16 tracks.
    Arm automation in Live and play the FX buttons: the moves are recorded in the arrangement.
  - Everything (patterns, song, kit, your own samples) is saved inside the Live set.
  - ⤓ MIDI saves the pattern or the whole song as a .mid file (to ~/Music/PULSAR); drag it onto a track.
  - To get audio: record PULSAR's track in Live (the WAV button is web-only).

ESPAÑOL
  Instalar: en Terminal pega   curl -fsSL https://drophlex.github.io/PULSAR/install.sh | bash
  En Ableton: dale play en Live y PULSAR sigue su tempo y su compás. Las notas MIDI C1–D#2 tocan los 16 pads.
  174 parámetros automatizables, incluidos los 16 efectos de performance como interruptores: arma la automatización
  en Live, toca los efectos y quedan grabados. Todo se guarda dentro del set de Live.
